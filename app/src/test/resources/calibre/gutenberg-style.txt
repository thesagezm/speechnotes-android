Chapter I. The Gate

It was a bright cold day in April, and the gates were striking
thirteen. The road wound north through the hills, past orchards
nobody owned anymore.

A farmer watched from the fence, chewing a straw that had seen
better summers.

Chapter II. The River

The river ran backwards that spring. Fish swam up the falls and
the mill turned against its own wheel, grinding nothing in
particular.

Chapter III. The Letter

Dear stranger, the letter began, though the handwriting was
unmistakably the reader's own. It spoke of debts that predated
the town.
